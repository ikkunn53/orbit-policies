Privacy, consent and deletion requests

Use this channel for questions about collection, withdrawal of consent, correction/deletion, access problems or security concerns. Requests are handled by the operator, not an automated form in the application.

  • The data or period concerned and a reply address. Do not post personal logs in public GitHub Issues.

Contact via X

Open the operator’s X profile above and use an available contact method. For a privacy or deletion request, first ask to arrange a private conversation. Do not include user IDs, server IDs or personal logs in public replies or posts. Use direct messages when available; an X account or login may be required.

Verification and handling

We may ask for proportionate proof of identity or server authority before disclosing or deleting data. Do not send passwords, bot tokens, OAuth tokens, two-factor codes or identity documents unless a separately agreed lawful verification process requires them. We will review the request without unnecessary delay, clarify its scope and explain any legal or security-based retention. If special-analysis consent is withdrawn, ask explicitly to stop future targeted collection as well as delete existing special-analysis data.

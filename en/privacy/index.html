Privacy Policy

1. Scope and operator

This policy applies to Orbit, operated by IKKUNN. It covers the Discord application, its restricted analytics website and this public policy site. Contact: https://x.com/ikkunn_53.

2. Data collected

  • Discord user, server, channel, role, thread and message identifiers; names, avatars, roles, membership changes and bot indicators.
  • Message metadata and counts, mentions and reply references; reaction counts, emoji and identifiers of reacting or replying users where needed for unique counts and moderation records.
  • Voice/stage channel joins, leaves, moves and connection durations; observable streaming/camera state and aggregate usage. We do not record voice audio, video or camera images.
  • Available invite codes, creators and estimated join/leave attribution; available role, thread, stage, scheduled-event, boost and moderation-event metadata.
  • For explicitly registered special-analysis targets only: accessible message text, message/channel IDs, timestamps and response metrics, after consent is obtained from the target.
  • Discord OAuth account/profile information, server authorization information, session cookies and operational/security logs needed to authenticate users and operate the service.

3. Purposes and limits

We use data for server administration, aggregate activity measurement, consent-based measurement of responses to a target’s posts, investigation of abuse and operation/security of the service. Individual activity logs and, where enabled, user-pair reply/reaction counts may be used for these administrative purposes. We do not use the data for advertising, sale, AI model training or inferring sensitive personal traits. Ordinary analytics does not save message text. The bot does not read private direct messages as part of this service.

4. Consent for special analysis

The operator must obtain and retain the target’s informed consent before registering them for message-text collection, including an explanation of the purpose, access scope and retention. Consent and withdrawal are handled by the operator; the application does not currently provide a self-service consent form. A target may withdraw consent through the contact channel. The operator will stop targeted collection and remove the associated special-analysis data after verifying the request. This does not automatically remove separate ordinary moderation records; these can be included in a deletion request. Server administrator permission is not a substitute for the target’s consent or Discord’s required intent approval.

5. Access, disclosure and service providers

Analytics and exports are restricted to the relevant server’s owner/Administrator accounts. The operator and infrastructure providers may process data as needed for operation, maintenance and security. Authorized administrators are responsible for protecting downloaded CSV copies. We do not publicly publish analytics or provide data to advertisers or data brokers. Disclosure may be necessary to comply with law or protect rights and safety. GitHub Pages hosts this policy site only; no analytics data is published here. GitHub may process connection information under its own privacy statement.

6. Retention and deletion

  • The retention baseline for dated activity, message text and reaction/reply snapshots is 400 days. Maintenance jobs remove expired records in batches; deletion may not occur at the exact expiry time.
  • Processed ingestion records have a seven-day cleanup baseline. Current identity/configuration records may remain while needed to operate the service or handle a request.
  • Managed database backups use a 14-day rotation baseline by default. Copies in backups are restricted, expire through rotation, and must not be reintroduced into active use after an accepted deletion request.
  • Removing a special-analysis target deletes its associated special-analysis records. Removing the bot or leaving a server does not by itself immediately erase every historical record. Request deletion through the operator.
  • If legal obligations or an ongoing security incident require limited retention, the operator will explain the applicable scope and reason where permitted.

7. Your requests

Contact https://x.com/ikkunn_53 to ask about your data, withdraw special-analysis consent or request correction/deletion. Include only the Discord user ID, relevant server ID, request scope and a way to reply. The operator will verify identity and authority using proportionate information, investigate and respond without unnecessary delay. Requests are currently handled manually, not through an automated deletion portal. We never ask for your password, bot token, OAuth token or two-factor backup codes.

8. Security and cookies

The analytics application uses access controls, restricted sessions and protected configuration. Authorized users must keep their devices and exports secure. Session cookies support login; this static policy site adds no advertising or analytics scripts or application login cookies. No security measure eliminates every risk.

9. Updates

We publish material changes on this site with an updated date and, when appropriate, provide an additional notice. New collection purposes will require review and any necessary notice or consent.

Terms of Service

1. Service and eligibility

Orbit, operated by IKKUNN, is a restricted server-administration analytics service. Use is limited to authorized server owners/Administrator accounts. Users must meet Discord’s age and eligibility requirements and comply with Discord’s applicable terms and policies. These terms do not grant access to any data or privileged intent that Discord does not authorize.

2. Administrator responsibilities

  • Explain the bot’s collection and purposes to server members and use only necessary settings and permissions.
  • Obtain and record the target’s informed consent before enabling special-analysis message-text collection; accept withdrawal and deletion requests.
  • Use logs and statistics only for the stated server-administration, abuse investigation and consent-based measurement purposes.
  • Protect administrator accounts, restrict access to exports, and do not publicly disclose personal logs or CSV data.

3. Prohibited use

  • Unauthorized access, permission bypasses, token sharing or attempts to obtain data from another server.
  • Harassment, stalking, doxxing, discrimination or inference of sensitive personal traits.
  • Selling Discord-derived data, advertising use, AI model training, or use prohibited by Discord.
  • Collecting message text for a target without the required consent or continuing targeted collection after an accepted withdrawal.
  • Bypassing Discord API rate limits, intent approvals or other technical restrictions.

4. Accuracy and availability

Data reflects observations, not a complete reconstruction of Discord history. Bot downtime, permissions, API limitations and unavailable past observations can cause missing or estimated values. Analytics is delayed by ten minutes. Invite attribution is an estimate, and response metrics do not prove a person’s intentions. Do not make serious disciplinary decisions based only on a ranking or automated metric; examine the underlying context. Features may change or be suspended for security, maintenance or Discord policy compliance.

5. Data and requests

Data handling is described in the Privacy Policy. Copyright and other rights in messages remain with their respective rights holders. These terms do not transfer ownership of user content. Contact the operator for consent withdrawal, data deletion or termination; uninstalling the bot alone does not immediately purge all records.
